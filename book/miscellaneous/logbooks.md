# Log books

Every manual comes with a log book entry. Fill it in here, in the browser: type your answers, add your graphs and photos, and export the PDF you hand in on Brightspace.

```{typst-overview}
:highlight-title: This week
:highlight-weekly: Week 3, 2026-11-16
```

## How it works

1. **Open your log book.** Your work is saved in this browser as you type, and it is there again when you come back.
2. **Fill it in.** Type in the boxes and use **Insert image** for graphs and photos. **Image page** and **Text page** add a page at the end.
3. **Export PDF** when you are done, and hand in that file on Brightspace.

Nothing is uploaded: your answers stay on your own computer until you hand in the PDF. The shield button in the log book deletes what this browser kept. The log book works in Chrome, Edge and Firefox.
