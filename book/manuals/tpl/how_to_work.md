
```{admonition} How To Work
:class: important
The **Preparatory Homework (BAS)** part of this manual should be completed **individually**.

The **Practicum (IC)** part should be completed **in pairs**. During the practicum, divide the workload as equally as possible, for example by letting one student build one part of the circuit while the other student builds the other part, and then swapping roles for measurements and checks.

Throughout this manual, keep your calculations, measurements, sketches, and thoughts in your log book entry. The comparison tables from Compare and Conclude should be completed in your log book entry and included in your onepager report for this lab.

Open the entry in the editor from the download menu at the top of this page, under **Log book**, or from the book **Log books** page. Complete the **Preparation** side before the practicum, then fill in the **Practicum** side at the bench. Type in the boxes, draw sketches, and add photos in the editor. Your work is saved in this browser as you go, so keep using the same browser; clearing your cookies or site data deletes it. Prefer your own PDF app, on a tablet for example? Choose **Download to keep filling in elsewhere (tablet, PDF app)** in the editor's **⋯** menu. Export the PDF from the editor when you are ready to hand it in.
```
